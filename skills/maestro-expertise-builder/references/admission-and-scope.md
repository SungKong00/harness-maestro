# Admission and scope

Admit a candidate only when all are true:

1. A concrete failure pattern is supported by a research artifact, repeated
   project evidence, or a critical omission with direct proof.
2. The candidate changes decisions or verification, not just tone or persona.
3. At least two future task shapes can reuse it.
4. Existing coverage has been checked.
5. The rules can name counterexamples and stop conditions.
6. A frozen evaluation can distinguish improvement from extra verbosity.

Choose the narrowest portability:

- `project`: depends on one repository's domain or constraints;
- `profile`: reusable across a known stack, platform, locale, or product class;
- `global`: independent of project details and supported by broadly applicable
  authority.

Frequent use does not make project-specific knowledge global. One successful
research run does not establish a reusable pack.

Prefer, in order:

1. deterministic checker or schema;
2. project Convention;
3. one compact Decision Guide in an existing pack;
4. a new Expertise Pack;
5. a specialist Executor only when distinct tools, permissions, or context
   isolation are required.
