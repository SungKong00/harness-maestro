# Competitor and reference analysis

## Purpose

Use current examples to understand category conventions and strategic gaps
without copying surface language or treating visibility as effectiveness.

## Decision checks

1. Select references by the same audience, job, lifecycle stage, and channel,
   not merely visual similarity.
2. Capture the exact artifact and inspection date.
3. Extract: category frame, primary promise, proof, offer, objection handling,
   CTA, and notable omission.
4. Separate an observed pattern from a claim about performance.
5. Classify transfer as required convention, useful hypothesis, incompatible
   constraint, or unproven style.
6. Compare at least one non-obvious alternative when the decision is strategic.

## Reject

Do not infer conversion, market leadership, or customer preference from a live
page. Do not reuse trademarked expression, unsupported claims, or a layout whose
content hierarchy solves a different problem.

## Output

Return a compact comparison matrix, transferable decisions with reasons,
rejected transfers, and open evidence questions.
