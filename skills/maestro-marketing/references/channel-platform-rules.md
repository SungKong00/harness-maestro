# Channel and platform rules

## Purpose

Adapt a validated core message to a current delivery surface.

## Decision checks

1. Confirm the current platform rule, format, audience state, and inspection
   date through authoritative guidance.
2. Preserve the offer and claim meaning while changing length and structure.
3. Match message depth to lifecycle stage: discovery, evaluation, conversion,
   onboarding, retention, or recovery.
4. Make sender identity, commercial intent, consent, and control clear where
   relevant.
5. Define fallback behavior for truncation, disabled media, small screens, and
   accessibility.
6. Keep tracking and personalization proportionate to consent and project
   policy.

## Stop conditions

Do not rely on remembered character limits or platform policy. Do not publish,
schedule, access an account, or spend budget. Escalate privacy, legal, or
deliverability constraints to the appropriate owner.
