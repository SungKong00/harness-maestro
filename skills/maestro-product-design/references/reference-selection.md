# Reference selection

Use references to discover and test decisions, not to import a surface style.

This guide is selected for explicit, bounded research intent such as `ui design
research`, `design reference research`, `ui reference research`, `find current
references`, `find ui design examples`, `find design examples`, `ui 디자인 리서치`, `디자인 레퍼런스 조사`,
`디자인 리서치`, `ui 디자인 사례 찾아`, `디자인 예시 찾아`, or `디자인 사례 찾아`.
Style adjectives alone do not open visual research.

## Search frame

Search the exact screen and platform pattern, such as `mobile checkout address`
or `desktop analytics dashboard`, rather than broad terms such as `beautiful
website`.

Collect a small diverse set that includes:

- exact-screen examples from shipped products for the same user task;
- one adjacent pattern that solves a similar information problem;
- one counterexample or failure mode;
- current platform or accessibility guidance when the decision depends on it.

Foundation, token, and standards documents can constrain a decision, but do not
count as shipped-screen references. Preserve a URL, a screenshot or observed
screen state, and access date for every shipped example. If the visible state or
responsive behavior cannot be observed, label that evidence `unverified`; do
not infer it from a desktop marketing image.

## Extract

For each reference record only:

- the product problem and context;
- the decision being observed;
- the observed screen or state evidence, or an explicit `unverified` label;
- the transferable decision and why it may transfer;
- the non-transferable decision because of brand, audience, content, or platform;
- source and access date.

Synthesize repeated patterns and meaningful disagreements. Popularity and visual
similarity are not evidence that a pattern fits this product.

## Apply and verify

Convert selected patterns into the current brief, design tokens, content, and
component boundaries. Compare before and after screenshots for hierarchy,
states, density, and responsive behavior. Preserve absent state or responsive
evidence as `unverified`. Keep URLs and screenshots in the research artifact,
not in every normal prompt.
