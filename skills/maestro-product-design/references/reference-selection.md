# Reference selection

## Purpose

Use current references to discover and test transferable product decisions
while preserving observed state, provenance, uncertainty, context, and local
validation instead of importing surface style.

## Use when

Use for explicit current-reference work and whenever a `standard` or `system`
assurance decision needs contextual evidence: new or forked primitives and
variants, changed action or information hierarchy, new token or visual
language, page redesign, external pattern transfer, or unresolved Korean and
accessibility behavior.

## Inputs

- exact product task, platform, user role, state, and decision to investigate;
- current brief, content, constraints, and design system;
- rights and privacy boundary for retained screenshots or recordings;
- local prototype and validation capacity.

## Decision checks

1. Frame the research question before searching. Search an exact screen and
   state rather than broad style adjectives.
2. Collect a bounded comparison set: one same-task shipped screen, one adjacent
   pattern, one counterexample or failure mode, and relevant standards or
   design-system constraints.
3. For each reference record URL, access date, product context, exact observed
   screen or state, screenshot or recording provenance, and `unverified` for
   behavior that was not observed.
4. Extract the transferable decision and its enabling context. Record
   non-transferable brand, audience, content, platform, permission, or scale.
5. Standards and component documentation constrain a decision but do not prove
   a shipped interaction state. Marketing images do not prove authenticated,
   responsive, loading, error, keyboard, or mobile behavior.
6. Apply only the decision to a local alternative using current content and
   tokens; compare it against the brief and representative task.
7. For each adopted component pattern, compare semantics, anatomy, variants,
   states, content limits, responsive behavior, localization, accessibility,
   and system lineage. Similar surface styling without the same contract is
   not a transferable component.
8. Convert research into exact claims and decision references. Preserve
   disagreement and rejected universal rules; source count is not a vote.

## Required evidence

- bounded research question and source plan;
- per-reference URL, access date, observed state evidence or unverified label,
  transferable decision, non-transferable context, and limitation;
- synthesis of agreement, disagreement, and counterexample;
- local prototype, comparison, or task observation for any adopted pattern.

## Reject or stop when

- style similarity or popularity is the only adoption reason;
- responsive or state behavior is inferred from a static marketing image;
- private or rights-restricted evidence would be copied into the core;
- no local test could distinguish whether the transferred decision fits.

## Verification

Render or prototype the adopted decision with representative local content,
states, widths, and interactions. Record what improved, what regressed, and
what remains unverified. Keep research URLs and observations in the bounded
artifact, not in every implementation prompt.

## Authority and evidence boundary

- **checklist** — `govuk-patterns`, `govuk-plan-user-research`,
  `govuk-analyse-research`, `govuk-prototyping`, and `uswds-principles`
  support question-led research, evidence-based pattern reuse or adaptation,
  documented findings, proportional prototypes, and continued validation.
- **derived checklist** — the required same-task shipped state, adjacent
  pattern, counterexample, and standards quartet is this Pack's bounded source
  plan. The sources do not prescribe that exact four-part set.
- **warning** — a component page or marketing image can constrain or inspire a
  decision but cannot attest unobserved authenticated, responsive, loading,
  error, keyboard, or mobile behavior.
- **current evidence gap** — no frozen evaluation artifact contains an actual
  shipped-state capture, access date, rights/privacy disposition, observed
  state, unverified state, transfer decision, and local comparison together.
  Four of five sources are from the GOV.UK ecosystem, so a publication review
  should test this protocol with independent product-research practice.

## Counterexamples

- A W3C standard is an authority constraint, not a shipped-screen reference.
- A component demo is not an end-to-end product task.
- GitHub Actions, Airflow, and Argo offer useful workflow decisions but differ
  in trace model, scale, and investigation task; their surfaces are not a theme.
