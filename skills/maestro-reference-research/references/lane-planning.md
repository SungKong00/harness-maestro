# Lane planning

Choose lanes from the uncertainty, not from a fixed team chart.

| Need | Lane |
|---|---|
| What is required or supported? | official |
| What does this codebase or a comparable implementation prove? | repository |
| What breaks in real operation? | field |
| What evidence would invalidate the attractive answer? | skeptic |

Depth limits:

- `quick`: one lane, one to three decisive sources;
- `normal`: up to two lanes, enough evidence to compare options;
- `deep`: up to three independent lanes for a high-impact or disputed choice.

A lane owns one source style and one question. It does not synthesize the whole
task. Prefer a single researcher unless parallel lanes reduce real latency or
preserve independent judgment.

Follow-up is allowed once only when the first synthesis records a material
conflict. Give the follow-up the conflict and evidence references, not every
lane transcript. Stop after it when no new evidence appears.
