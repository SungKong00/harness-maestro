# Adoption rubric

Adoption is a handoff recommendation, not automatic project authority.

| Adoption | Use when |
|---|---|
| `rule` | Tier 0/1 evidence supports a stable reusable requirement |
| `checklist` | Evidence supports a concrete verification or review step |
| `warning` | A material failure mode or transfer limit should stay visible |
| `optional-pattern` | Evidence supports a useful but context-dependent choice |
| `reject` | Evidence contradicts the claim or its project transfer |
| `open-question` | Authority or applicability remains insufficient |

Evaluate:

1. source authority and freshness;
2. claim verification status;
3. exact applicability;
4. project fit and conflicts with local authority;
5. failure cost if wrong;
6. whether a narrower checklist is safer than a rule.

Checklist candidates must be concrete and verifiable. Preserve rejected claim
IDs and reasons; otherwise future work may repeat weak research. Unknown claims
remain open or rejected. Contested claims may be adopted only when the
conflict, qualification, and verification action remain explicit.

Research never writes the Convention or Expertise Pack directly. Maestro may
apply a claim to the current Assignment, and the Expertise Builder may later
compile candidates through frozen evaluation and owner-governed lifecycle.
