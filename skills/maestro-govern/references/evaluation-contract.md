# Evaluation contract

Compare the current and candidate workflow on the same stratified, frozen cases. Pin the base revision, model policy, tools, budget, conventions, and adapter versions.

Measure:

- outcome and verifier result;
- correction and rollback count;
- unique decision-changing evidence;
- duplicate packet rate;
- parent-context tokens and total-system tokens separately;
- wall time and tool calls;
- human correction required.

Use raw artifacts and hide the intended answer from the evaluator. A candidate may be promoted only after forward tests, regression checks, cost review, independent verification, and owner approval.
